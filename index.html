Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the fare calculated for Laguna & Batangas?
Accent = ₱35/km, Avanza = ₱45/km, Montero = ₱50/km, fuel included. For pickups outside Cavite, a 50% vehicle-positioning fee covers the drive to your pickup. Toll fees are added at estimated cost. A branded PDF quotation is generated instantly after booking.
How much are the Tagaytay & Manila day tours?
Day tours are flat-rate: 8–10 hours with driver. Accent = ₱5,000/day, Avanza = ₱6,500/day, Montero = ₱7,000/day. Fuel, toll fees and parking are charged to the client at actual cost, not included in the flat rate.
Do you serve Laguna and Batangas?
Yes! We offer airport transfers from Laguna (Santa Rosa, Calamba, San Pablo, Biñan) and Batangas (Lipa, Tanauan, Batangas City, Nasugbu) to NAIA and vice versa, with instant live fare estimates.
Are toll fees and parking included?
Fuel is included in the per-km rate for airport transfers. Toll fees and parking are paid by the client at actual cost and shown as a separate line in your estimate and PDF quotation. For day tours, fuel, toll and parking are all charged to the client at actual cost.
What if my flight is delayed?
No problem. Include your flight number in the booking and we monitor arrival times. We wait for delayed flights at no extra charge within reasonable windows.
Do you offer child seats?
Child seats can be arranged upon request — just mention it in the Notes field of your booking. Children count as one passenger regardless of age.
What is your cancellation policy?
Free cancellation up to 24 hours before pickup. Last-minute changes may incur a small fee. Contact us via WhatsApp or Viber for the fastest response.
