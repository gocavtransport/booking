Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the fare calculated?
Every trip is priced dynamically: base charge + loaded distance (₱28/34/40 per km by vehicle) + vehicle positioning from our home base in General Trias (if pickup is outside our base zone) + estimated toll + airport fee (NAIA/Clark) + 12% night surcharge (22:00–06:00). Corporate accounts get 8% off when you return the same day. Fuel is always included.
Do you serve all of Metro Manila?
Yes — Makati, BGC, Manila, Quezon City, Pasig, Mandaluyong, Parañaque, Pasay, Las Piñas, Muntinlupa, Marikina, San Juan, Caloocan, Valenzuela, Malabon, Navotas, plus all of Cavite, Laguna, Batangas, and Clark Airport. All locations produce an instant live estimate.
How do corporate accounts work?
Tick the "Corporate Account" box when booking. You'll receive 8% off when you return the same day, multi-day site-visit loyalty (5% for 2 days, 8% for 3–7 days on the day-rate portion), priority dispatch, and consolidated weekly/monthly invoicing (select "Corporate Billing (Invoice)" as payment method). Perfect for airport transfers and full-day or multi-day site visits.
What's included in full-day rental?
8 hours and 100 km total, with driver and fuel included. The client is returned to the original pickup point. Excess distance is billed per km and excess hours per hour by vehicle class. Two-way toll fees are estimated in your quote and charged at actual cost, along with parking. Pickup at our home base gets an additional discount.
What is Multi-Day Site Visit?
For corporate clients who need the same route over consecutive days (e.g. audits or plant visits). Each day is a full-day package (8 hrs / 100 km), and the client is returned to the original pickup point every day. Book 2–7 days. Corporate loyalty: 5% off day rates for 2 days, 8% for 3–7 days. Two-way toll × number of days is estimated; excess km/hours billed if you exceed the daily package. Same driver/vehicle preferred subject to availability.
Are toll fees and parking included?
Fuel is included. Toll fees are estimated in your live quote and charged at actual cost. For full-day and multi-day site visits, toll is estimated two-way (and × days for multi-day). Parking is paid by the client at actual cost. Both are shown as separate line items in your estimate and PDF quotation.
What if my flight is delayed?
No problem. Include your flight number and we monitor arrival times. We wait for delayed flights at no extra charge within reasonable windows.
What is your cancellation policy?
Free cancellation up to 24 hours before pickup. 12–24 hours before: 25% of estimated fare (minimum ₱500). Less than 12 hours or no-show: 50%–100% of estimated fare. Multi-day corporate site visits: free cancel of the whole block until 24 hours before Day 1; after Day 1 starts, unused later days can be dropped free if you notify us before 8:00 PM the evening before (otherwise 50% of that day may apply). Reschedules with 12+ hours notice are usually free once. Flight delays with a given flight number are monitored at no extra charge within reasonable windows. Contact us via WhatsApp or Viber for the fastest response.
