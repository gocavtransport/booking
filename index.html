Terms & Accounts

Payment terms by service

Corporate bookings are billed by service type. BPI or BDO is preferred. GCash or Maya is fine for smaller or urgent trips. Toll and parking, if any, are billed at actual cost on top of the estimate.

One-way transfer
Airport and point-to-point drops are billed after the ride. Please settle within 3 days. Fuel is included; toll and parking are added at actual cost.
Same-day / Split return
Pickup and return on the same or next day. Split round-trips receive a 10% loyalty discount on the return trip. Please settle within 3 days after the trip.
Multi-day round trip
For drop-off now and pickup on a later date. A 50% reservation holds the vehicle. The remaining balance is due after the return trip, plus actual toll and parking.
Full-day / site visit
Covers 8 hours and 100 km, with the client returned to pickup. We send the invoice after the visit. Payment is due within 7 days. Extra hours, extra km, toll, and parking are billed separately at actual.
Out-of-Town Multi-Day (Vehicle Retained)
Driver stays with you (e.g., 3 days in Baguio). A ₱1,200/night driver allowance is automatically added so they can secure lodging/meals. 50% reservation required. Tolls are charged once for the round trip.
Multi-day site visit (Same Route)
Returned to pickup daily. Corporate accounts receive one invoice for the full block, due within 7 days. Corporate loyalty is 5% for 2 days and 8% for 3–7 days. New clients: 50% before Day 1.

Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the fare calculated?
Every trip is priced dynamically: base charge + loaded distance (₱28/34/40 per km by vehicle) + vehicle positioning (₱23/km from our home base in General Trias if pickup is outside our base zone) + estimated toll by preferred expressway + 15% night surcharge (22:00–06:00). Corporate accounts get 8% off when returning on standby. Fuel is always included.
Do you serve all of Metro Manila and Luzon?
Yes — we cover Metro Manila, Cavite, Laguna, Batangas, Rizal, Bulacan, Pampanga (Clark/Subic), Tarlac, La Union (Baguio gateway), and Quezon province. All locations produce an instant live estimate with regional expressway tolls.
How do corporate accounts work?
Tick the "Corporate Account" box when booking. You'll receive 8% off when you return the same day, multi-day site-visit loyalty (5% for 2 days, 8% for 3–7 days on the day-rate portion), priority dispatch, and consolidated weekly/monthly invoicing (select "Corporate Billing (Invoice)" as payment method). Perfect for airport transfers and full-day or multi-day site visits.
What's included in full-day rental?
8 hours and 100 km total, with driver and fuel included. The client is returned to the original pickup point. Excess distance is billed per km and excess hours per hour by vehicle class. Two-way toll fees are estimated in your quote and charged at actual cost, along with parking. Pickup at our home base gets an additional discount.
What is Multi-Day Site Visit vs. Out-of-Town?
"Multi-Day Site Visit" is for clients who need the same route over consecutive days (e.g. daily audits), returned to pickup daily. "Out-of-Town" is when the driver stays with the client (e.g., 3 days in Baguio). For Out-of-Town trips, a ₱1,200/night allowance is automatically added for the driver to secure their own lodging and meals.
Are toll fees and parking included?
Fuel is included. Toll fees are estimated in your live quote according to your route preference (Elevated/Fastest, Standard, or Economical) and charged at actual cost. Parking is paid by the client at actual cost.
What if my flight is delayed?
No problem. Include your flight number and we monitor arrival times. We wait for delayed flights at no extra charge within reasonable windows.
What about waiting time and flight delays?
Airport pickups: 45 minutes free waiting from the time we notify you that the driver is on stand. Extra waiting is billed per 30 minutes. If you give a flight number, we track the flight and adjust pickup for delays at no extra charge within a reasonable window. Full-day / site visit: 8 hours included; time beyond that is billed at the vehicle excess-hour rate. Night trips (22:00–06:00) have a +15% surcharge.
What is your cancellation policy?
Free cancellation up to 24 hours before pickup. 12–24 hours before: 25% of estimated fare (minimum ₱500). Less than 12 hours or no-show: 50%–100% of estimated fare. Multi-day corporate site visits: free cancel of the whole block until 24 hours before Day 1; after Day 1 starts, unused later days can be dropped free if you notify us before 8:00 PM the evening before. Reschedules with 12+ hours notice are usually free once.
