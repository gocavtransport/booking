Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you serve Laguna and Batangas?
Yes! We offer airport transfers from Laguna (Santa Rosa, Calamba, San Pablo, Biñan) and Batangas (Lipa, Tanauan, Batangas City, Nasugbu) to NAIA and vice versa. These are custom-quoted — submit a booking and we'll send the rate to your email.
How do I confirm my booking?
After you send your booking via Email, WhatsApp, or Viber, our team will confirm within 24 hours. You'll receive a reference number — keep it for your records.
Are toll fees and parking included?
For Cavite airport transfers, fuel is included but toll fees and parking are paid by the client (actual cost). For Laguna/Batangas transfers and day tours, fuel, toll, and parking are itemized in your custom quotation.
What if my flight is delayed?
No problem. Include your flight number in the booking and we monitor arrival times. We wait for delayed flights at no extra charge within reasonable windows.
Do you offer child seats?
Child seats can be arranged upon request — just mention it in the Notes field of your booking. Children count as one passenger regardless of age.
What is your cancellation policy?
Free cancellation up to 24 hours before pickup. Last-minute changes may incur a small fee. Contact us via WhatsApp or Viber for the fastest response.
